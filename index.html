Upon purchase of DigiSpherEMR V2, DigiSpher Health Systems grants the licensee a perpetual, non-exclusive, non-transferable lifetime license to install and operate the software for private practice management on the number of modern Windows PCs included in the purchased license: one or two.

DigiSpherEMR operates as a 100% offline application storing data locally in a SQLite database. The licensee maintains full, unencumbered ownership of all patient records and is responsible for maintaining local security, hardware backups, and access credentials.

Privacy Policy & Data Security

1. Zero Cloud Data Transmission

DigiSpherEMR is engineered on a strict offline-first architecture. All patient health information (PHI), clinical SOAP notes, prescriptions, appointments, and practice financial records remain on your local hard drive and are never uploaded to cloud servers.

2. No Telemetry or Usage Tracking

We do not track, collect, sell, or analyze your clinical data, patient counts, financial revenue, or application usage. Your practice operations remain completely confidential and sovereign.

3. Doctor Privacy Switch

DigiSpherEMR includes a built-in 1-click Privacy Switch that instantly masks patient surnames and identifying details during screen sharing or patient-facing consultations.

4. Support Communications

When contacting technical support via email, any provided diagnostic information is used strictly to resolve your issue and is never shared with third parties.
